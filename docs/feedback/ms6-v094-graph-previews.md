Build v0.94. This file covers only the graph, preview and navigation items of `Tegg v 094 feedback.md`. Other sessions
own Search (PR #98), the Tasks views and the date picker (PR #96), and the type-page columns. Triage as in
`ms5-v052.md`: **bug** means the build contradicts an existing AC, decision or design board; **change** means new
behaviour and needs a D-xx. Decisions are D-1164…D-1199; points are AP-994 onward.

|---|---|---|---|

## Points for the owner

| Point | Question | Proposal | Alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| AP-994 | **Hover becomes the default (F2).** FR-LNK-12 and D-923 default to `click`, 400 ms. | `preview.mode` defaults to `hover` and `preview.hoverDelay` to 1,000 ms; the range stays 200–1,500 ms. A value already stored on the machine stays, so a Mac where Settings › Previews was changed keeps its choice. The graph's hover ring (FR-GRP-06) fills over the same 1,000 ms. FR-LNK-12's wording, the Settings order (`Hover to preview, click to open` first) and FR-SET-07 AC-1…AC-3 change. Tests that rely on the default set `preview.mode = click` (D-949's rule) under one approved AC-test change | (b) also reset a stored `click` to `hover` once (a migration) |
| AP-995 | **A click inside a preview opens the page (F1).** What counts as "inside"? | A primary click on the preview's header, strip or body opens the previewed object in the current view (with its `#Heading` / `#^id`), as `Open` does, in every mode and in the graph's preview column. These keep their own job: links and embed cards in the preview (AP-1000), `Close preview`, `Back to <title>`, the resize handle, the scrollbar, a drag that selects text (no open after a selection), and ⌘-click (D-925). The keyboard is unchanged (⏎ on `Open`). Supersedes D-942's "a click inside a hover preview makes it a clicked preview" | (b) only a click on the header (icon, title, type) opens; a click in the body keeps the focus inside as now |
| AP-996 | **No preview of embeds (F3).** Today a click on a card or link-mode embed, or on a full embed's header, shows a preview (FR-LNK-09, previews.md FR-LNK-09). | Embeds don't preview in any mode. A click on a card or link-mode embed, or on a full embed's header, opens the target (as D-423 before previews), and hover does nothing on them. The link menu's `Show preview` and ⌥Space still preview an embed on request. Embeds inside a preview stay cards (D-933). FR-LNK-09 becomes W for v1.0 | (b) keep previews of card and link embeds, drop only the full embed's header preview, whose content is already shown |
| AP-997 | **One edge style (F4).** | Every edge is a 1.5 px solid line in `--muted`, tag edges included. D-902's "strongest kind" no longer sets the style, but one line per pair stays, and the kinds are still named in the preview's connection line, VoiceOver and the List view. The `Connections` filters stay; their line samples go, leaving the checkbox and the label (R108, board `Graph` `STY`, FR-GRP-01 AC-3 and the R108 style column change; D-973 goes) | (b) keep tag edges dotted, so tags stay distinct from objects |
| AP-998 | **Horizontal scroll never navigates (F5).** D-165 maps a two-finger horizontal swipe to Back / Forward. | Remove the wheel-based swipe from the web UI: horizontal wheel and trackpad scrolling only scroll. Back / Forward keep the title-bar buttons, ⌘[ / ⌘], the palette, the mouse's back and forward buttons, and the native navigation-swipe events the app's monitor gets (three-finger swipe, mouse utilities such as Logi Options+ that send the buttons as swipes, 5B feedback F1). FR-NAV-03 AC-3 (manual) changes | (b) also drop the native swipe events: only the buttons and keys navigate |
| AP-999 | **Scrolling pans the graph (F5).** Today a plain wheel over the canvas does nothing; ⌘-wheel zooms, a drag pans. | In the full view and the panel, a plain wheel or two-finger scroll pans the canvas in both directions by its deltas (no zoom); ⌘-wheel and a trackpad pinch zoom around the pointer; it still hides a hover preview (AP-941). At the canvas's pan limits the page doesn't scroll | (b) only horizontal scroll pans; vertical scroll keeps scrolling the page or panel |
| AP-1000 | **Links inside a preview (with AP-994, AP-995).** D-932: a click on a link in a preview replaces the content in place, with `Back to <title>`. | Unchanged in `click` mode. In `hover` mode a link in a preview behaves like any reference: hover replaces the content in place after the delay (D-932's Back); a click opens the link's target | (b) a click on a link in a preview always replaces in place, in both modes |
| AP-1001 | **Where the follow-up lands.** | One slice on top of G3 in the panel (PR #99): ACs in `docs/acceptance/previews.md` and `graph.md` (new criteria plus the changed ones listed with each point), red tests first, then the build. The real-app check of AP-998 stays manual (D-170) | Split: previews (AP-994…AP-996, AP-1000) and graph or navigation (AP-997…AP-999) as two PRs |
