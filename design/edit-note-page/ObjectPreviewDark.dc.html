Hover mode (D-917, D-942)

Termín drží Projekt Alfa do konce roku.

  • The same preview, without the focus ring; the focus stays in the text.
  • The delay (400 ms, 200–1,500 ms in Settings) counts from the last pointer move; typing or scrolling cancels it.
  • Moving onto the preview keeps it; leaving both hides it after 300 ms.
  • A click inside makes it a clicked preview. Footer: “Click or ⌘↩ to open”.

A click in editable text (D-926)

Viz Rozpočet 2027 a další poznámky.

The click previews the chip and leaves the caret right after it, so typing goes on after Esc and ⌫ removes the link whole. A click beside a chip places the caret as in any text. In the editor ⏎ stays a text key; ⌥Space at a chip previews, elsewhere it types a no-break space.
