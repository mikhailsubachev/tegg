Mindmap block · FR-MMP-01…03

Mindmap layouts, editing and navigation

The mindmap block gets a layout switch with two icons. Right is the current layout. Spider puts the root in the centre with branches on both sides. Same map, same list in the file; only the drawing changes. The layout name is kept in the callout line (> [!mindmap|spider]), nothing else is stored.

A · Branches to the right (current, default)

Root on the left · reads like an outline
Layout switch, first option selected
  • The root sits on the left; every branch grows to the right in outline order.
  • Best for deep, list-like maps and narrow note columns: the width grows only with depth.

B · Spider: branches on both sides

Root in the centre · first-level branches split left and right
Layout switch, second option selected
  • The root sits in the middle. First-level branches alternate right, left, right, left in outline order, so the sides stay balanced (Agenda and Travel right, Venue and Team activities left).
  • Children always grow away from the root: leaves on the left side are placed to the left of their parent.
  • Keyboard: Tab makes a child, ↩ a sibling (section D). ← and → follow the screen: on a left-side node ← is the first child and → the parent; on the root → is the first right-side branch, ← the first left-side branch. ↑ and ↓ move among siblings and continue to the nearest node at the same depth under the next parent (cousin). A new first-level branch appended at the end lands on the lighter side, because the sides alternate by outline position.
  • If the map is wider than the note column, the block scrolls sideways (full screen is v1.0).

C · The switch

Per mindmap block · shown in Map and List, hidden in the read view
  • Two icon buttons in the block header, left of Map | List; tooltips “Branches to the right” and “Spider: branches on both sides”.
  • A radio group for VoiceOver (“Mindmap layout, Spider, selected, 2 of 2”).
  • The choice is per block and changes only how the map is drawn, never the list.
  • Where the choice lives: Obsidian callout metadata on the callout line, > [!mindmap|spider] Title. Obsidian renders the callout and ignores the metadata. right is the default and is never written; switching back removes |spider. Unknown metadata means right and is preserved byte for byte. A switch rewrites that one line and is one undo step.

D · Editing on the map

Map view · keyboard first · every key is the same list edit the List view would make, one undo step
↩New sibling after the node, edited at once. On the root: first child. On a synthesized root: a new top-level item at the end.
TabNew child at the end of the node’s children, edited at once.
F2 · double-clickEdit the node’s text: a single-line field with the item’s Markdown source ([[Lisbon]], #tag, **bold** as written). ↩ commits, Esc cancels, Tab commits and adds a child.
⌫ · DeleteRemoves a node without children; focus goes to the previous sibling, else the parent. A node with children is not removed on the map (use List).
SpaceFold / unfold a node with children (nothing on a leaf). → on a folded node unfolds it; Tab on it unfolds, then adds the child.
↑ · ↓Previous / next sibling; past the first or last, the nearest node at the same depth under the previous / next parent.
⌥↑ · ⌥↓Move the node among its siblings.
⌥→ · ⌥←Nest under the previous sibling · un-nest after the parent (Tab / ⇧Tab of the list).
⌘↩Open the node’s first link (unchanged).
Click · double-clickClick focuses the node; double-click edits its text; a click on a chip opens its link; drag moves the branch (section F).
⌘Z · ⇧⌘ZUndo and redo work with the map focused. The map stays in view after every edit, focus on the edited node.
Not on the map yet (v1.0): zoom and pan, [[ and # autocomplete, checklist toggle, node → object, full screen. Checklist nodes stay read-only on the map.

E · Fold and add with the mouse

Agenda focused (hovered) · Travel folded
  • Fold control on the connector’s elbow of a node with children. Open: shown on hover or focus with − (“Collapse Agenda”). Folded: always shown, with the hidden count (+3, “Expand Travel, 3 hidden”). A click toggles; so does Space.
  • Add child (+, accent) at the node’s outer edge on hover or focus: adds an empty child at the end and edits it, as Tab. On a leaf it sits where the connector would start. The spider root has one, on its right edge.
  • Folds are not remembered: reopening the note shows the map unfolded. Folding writes nothing.
  • In the read view the fold control works; there is no + .

F · Drag and drop

Team activities dragged onto Agenda’s lower half: it becomes the sibling after Agenda
upper half: sibling before
lower half: sibling after
outer third:
last child
Drop zones of a target node (right side; mirrored on the spider’s left side)
  • Drag starts after a 4 px move, so a click still only focuses. The node moves with its whole subtree; its old place shows a dashed outline.
  • Over a node: upper / lower half → sibling before / after (an accent line); outer third → its last child (an accent ring on the target). No drop into its own subtree; the root can’t be dragged.
  • Hovering 600 ms over a folded node unfolds it. Esc, or a drop on empty space, cancels.
  • A drop is one list edit (the same bytes as ⌥↑ ⌥↓ ⌥→ ⌥←), one undo step; focus stays on the moved node. Not in the read view.
