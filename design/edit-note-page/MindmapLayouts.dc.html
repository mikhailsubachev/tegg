Mindmap block · FR-MMP-01…03

Mindmap layouts: right and spider

The mindmap block gets a layout switch with two icons. Right is the current layout. Spider puts the root in the centre with branches on both sides. Same map, same list in the file; only the drawing changes. The layout name is kept in the callout line (> [!mindmap|spider]), nothing else is stored.

A · Branches to the right (current, default)

Root on the left · reads like an outline
Layout switch, first option selected
  • The root sits on the left; every branch grows to the right in outline order.
  • Best for deep, list-like maps and narrow note columns: the width grows only with depth.

B · Spider: branches on both sides

Root in the centre · first-level branches split left and right
Layout switch, second option selected
  • The root sits in the middle. First-level branches alternate right, left, right, left in outline order, so the sides stay balanced (Agenda and Travel right, Venue and Team activities left).
  • Children always grow away from the root: leaves on the left side are placed to the left of their parent.
  • Keyboard: Tab makes a child, ↩ a sibling (section D). ← and → follow the screen: on a left-side node ← is the first child and → the parent; on the root → is the first right-side branch, ← the first left-side branch. ↑ and ↓ stay outline order. A new first-level branch appended at the end lands on the lighter side, because the sides alternate by outline position.
  • If the map is wider than the note column, the block scrolls sideways (full screen is v1.0).

C · The switch

Per mindmap block · shown in Map and List, hidden in the read view
  • Two icon buttons in the block header, left of Map | List; tooltips “Branches to the right” and “Spider: branches on both sides”.
  • A radio group for VoiceOver (“Mindmap layout, Spider, selected, 2 of 2”).
  • The choice is per block and changes only how the map is drawn, never the list.
  • Where the choice lives: Obsidian callout metadata on the callout line, > [!mindmap|spider] Title. Obsidian renders the callout and ignores the metadata. right is the default and is never written; switching back removes |spider. Unknown metadata means right and is preserved byte for byte. A switch rewrites that one line and is one undo step.

D · Editing on the map

Map view · keyboard first · every key is the same list edit the List view would make, one undo step
↩New sibling after the node, edited at once. On the root: first child. On a synthesized root: a new top-level item at the end.
TabNew child at the end of the node’s children, edited at once.
Space · F2Edit the node’s text: a single-line field with the item’s Markdown source ([[Lisbon]], #tag, **bold** as written). ↩ commits, Esc cancels, Tab commits and adds a child.
⌫ · DeleteRemoves a node without children; focus goes to the previous sibling, else the parent. A node with children is not removed on the map (use List).
⌥↑ · ⌥↓Move the node among its siblings.
⌥→ · ⌥←Nest under the previous sibling · un-nest after the parent (Tab / ⇧Tab of the list).
⌘↩Open the node’s first link (unchanged).
Click · double-clickClick focuses the node (it no longer switches to List); double-click edits its text; a click on a chip opens its link.
⌘Z · ⇧⌘ZUndo and redo work with the map focused. The map stays in view after every edit, focus on the edited node.
Not on the map yet (v1.0): drag to re-parent, collapse, zoom and pan, [[ and # autocomplete, checklist toggle, node → object, full screen. Checklist nodes stay read-only on the map.
