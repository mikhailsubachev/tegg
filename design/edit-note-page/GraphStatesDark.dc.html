PanelThe panel never draws more than 60 nodes. Over that it offers a lower depth or the full view.
  • Full view caps at 150 nodes (most links first, the centre and depth-1 always kept). Above the cap: a banner with the next lower depth and its count, Filter types, or Show all.
  • Labels fade out above 60 visible nodes (FR-GRP-05) and come back on zoom or hover.
  • Show all draws everything once; it is not remembered.
